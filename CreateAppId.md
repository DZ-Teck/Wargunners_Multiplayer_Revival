To create your custom App ID, you need to create an account on Photon Engine:

https://id.photonengine.com/account/signup

Once your account is created, go to the "Dashboard" tab and create a new app:

then, Select Photon SDK to "Realtime"

Name your project whatever you like. For example, "Wargunners-Revival"

Press the button "Create".


You have now created your App ID. You can return to the tutorial to add it to your game!ir au tutoriel pour l'ajouter dans votre jeu !
